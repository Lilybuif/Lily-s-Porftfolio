AYSA Youth Sports Website - Design Leadership Reflection

Project Duration: 4 Weeks Role: Design Lead Team Size: 4 Members
CASE STUDY LEADERSHIP UI/UX DESIGN

Duration

4 weeks

Team Size

4 members (Design Lead)

Tools Used

Figma, Adobe XD, Project Management Tools, Communication Platforms

Project Overview

This reflection explores my experience leading the redesign of AYSA Kent Academy's youth sports website. As the design lead for a team of four, I was responsible for coordinating research, design decisions, and stakeholder communication throughout the project lifecycle.

Leadership Challenges

Leading a design team presented several challenges, including balancing individual creative visions with project constraints, managing stakeholder expectations, and ensuring consistent communication across team members. I learned that effective leadership requires both technical expertise and strong interpersonal skills.

Key Learnings

The project reinforced several important lessons about design leadership:

  • Clear communication is essential for team alignment and project success
  • Stakeholder management requires patience and active listening
  • Technical implementation challenges often require creative problem-solving
  • Iterative improvement is more valuable than perfect initial solutions

Communication Strategies

I discovered that successful project communication involves regular check-ins, clear documentation, and adapting communication styles to different stakeholders. Weekly team meetings and daily stand-ups helped maintain momentum and address issues quickly.

Technical Implementation

The technical challenges of implementing the design revealed the importance of understanding development constraints early in the process. Working closely with developers helped identify potential issues and find optimal solutions that balanced design goals with technical feasibility.

Stakeholder Management

Managing relationships with AYSA Kent Academy stakeholders taught me the value of building trust through transparency and delivering on promises. Regular updates and involving stakeholders in key decisions helped maintain their confidence in the project.

Team Dynamics

Leading a diverse team highlighted the importance of recognizing individual strengths and creating an environment where everyone feels valued and heard. Encouraging collaboration while providing clear direction helped maximize team effectiveness.

Future Applications

The leadership skills developed during this project will be valuable in future design roles. I now understand the importance of balancing creative vision with practical constraints, and how effective communication can make the difference between project success and failure.

Conclusion

This project was a valuable learning experience that strengthened my design leadership capabilities. The challenges encountered and lessons learned will inform my approach to future projects and help me become a more effective design leader.
